# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance activities and coordinate acceptance testing. They collaborate with product and engineering to define test strategies and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria alignment
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Coordinate user acceptance testing (UAT) with stakeholders
- Identify and document defects and quality issues
- Ensure coverage of critical flows and edge cases

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production issues
- Maintain quality standards and user confidence
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test automation, review technical design for testability
- **Product Manager**: Align on acceptance criteria and success metrics
- **Project Manager**: Communicate testing progress and timeline impacts

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA kickoffs
- Test case documentation and defect reports
- UAT coordination with stakeholders

---

## Tech Lead / Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Tech Leads provide technical direction, mentor developers, and own architectural decisions. They ensure technical excellence and long-term system health while supporting team growth and capability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design standards
- Review and mentor developers on code quality and design
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor junior developers and support skill growth
- Collaborate with Product Manager on technical trade-offs

### Goals
- Maintain high code quality and system reliability
- Support team capability and career development
- Enable scalable, maintainable solutions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide mentorship, code review guidance, and technical direction
- **Product Manager**: Advise on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- **Project Manager**: Identify technical blockers and dependencies early

### Typical Communication
- Code reviews and architectural discussions
- 1-on-1s with developers
- Technical planning sessions and design reviews

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects adhere to security policies, regulatory requirements, and risk management standards. They work throughout the project lifecycle to embed security best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and implementations for security vulnerabilities
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and company standards
- Coordinate security scanning and penetration testing
- Review and approve security exceptions
- Educate team on secure coding practices

### Goals
- Reduce security risks and compliance violations
- Build security into delivery processes from the start
- Maintain customer trust and meet regulatory obligations

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Advise on secure coding and design patterns
- **Product Manager**: Identify compliance requirements early in planning
- **Project Manager**: Ensure security activities are included in project timeline

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints during planning and implementation
- Incident response coordination
- Security training and awareness sessions

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads own user experience strategy, interaction design, and design system consistency. They collaborate with product and engineering to ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define interaction patterns
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility compliance and inclusive design
- Maintain design system consistency
- Collaborate with developers on implementation quality

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Reduce support burden through better UX

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on implementation details and design feasibility
- **Product Manager**: Align on user needs and feature prioritization
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Define UX acceptance criteria and usability testing

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions and critique
- User research findings and design specifications
- Accessibility and usability testing coordination

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team toward higher velocity and continuous improvement. They support the team's adoption of agile practices and help maintain a sustainable pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, retrospectives, and reviews
- Identify and remove blockers preventing team progress
- Coach team on agile practices and ceremonies
- Track and coach team velocity and capacity
- Support process improvements and team culture

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, predictable delivery
- Foster psychological safety and team engagement
- Continuously improve team processes and efficiency

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate on timeline and resource management
- **Developers**: Support in adopting agile practices and removing blockers
- **Product Manager**: Help prioritize backlog and manage sprint scope

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all team ceremonies
- One-on-ones and coaching conversations
- Process improvement discussions

---

## Stakeholder / Business Owner

### Role Summary
Business Owners represent key stakeholders and provide business context, approval authority, and ROI oversight for projects. They ensure initiatives align with business strategy and monitor return on investment.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Approve scope changes and trade-offs
- Monitor project ROI and business metrics
- Communicate project status to executive leadership
- Remove business-level blockers and make priority decisions

### Goals
- Maximize business value and return on investment
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable informed decision-making on scope and resources

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Align on strategic priorities and success metrics
- **Project Manager**: Review progress, approve scope changes, and escalate risks
- **Developers**: Provide business context and answer domain questions

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive updates
- Scope change approvals and priority decisions
- Business metric reviews and ROI assessments

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When managing projects, reference these roles to ensure all key perspectives are represented in planning and decision-making.
